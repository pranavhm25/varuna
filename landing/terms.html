Terms

Research use, stated plainly.

Last updated 23 November 2026

Varuna is an academic machine learning project. It is not an official drought declaration, a government advisory or a substitute for a district officer's judgement.

Data and outputs

Rainfall records come from Karnataka State Natural Disaster Monitoring Centre annual tables, archived by the OpenCity Karnataka portal and released under the Open Data Commons Attribution licence. Departures, risk zones, cluster labels and model outputs are research results derived from those records.

No warranty

The project is provided as it is, without warranty of any kind. Evaluation shows that the selected classifier recovers 6.06% of drought events in the 2025 held out season. Verify any figure against the source record before using it for an operational decision.

Limits worth knowing

  • Predictions use surface rainfall only. Soil moisture, temperature, evapotranspiration and groundwater are not inputs.
  • 338 stations carry static baseline normals that can turn a wet season into a deficit label. They are flagged in the interface.
  • The source tables contain no coordinates, so the project does not draw maps.
  • Calendar year 2023 was not published at hobli level and is not imputed.

Reuse

You may reuse the data under the Open Data Commons Attribution licence with attribution to KSNDMC and OpenCity Karnataka. The project source is public on GitHub; check the repository for its current licence before reusing code. Cite Varuna if you reuse the analysis.
